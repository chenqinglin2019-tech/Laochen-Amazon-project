# Trademark, trade dress, copyright, and character rules

Extract words from brand, title, manufacturer, model, series, slogans, packaging OCR, logos, and authorization statements. Search exact, punctuation/spacing variants, misspellings, phonetic forms, translations, and transliterations. Compare office, status, owner, Nice class, goods/services, similarity, and market context.

Treat figurative marks, product shape, packaging layout, repeated patterns, and stable color/shape combinations as separate surfaces. Do not call a design trade dress only because it is distinctive; record evidence of source identification or market recognition.

For each copyright/character asset, record source image, OCR/notices, SHA-256, earliest located source, owner/creator claim, license scope/territory/term, commercial and sublicensing rights, reverse-image evidence, and optional registration evidence. Copyright can exist without registration. Unknown provenance for a central asset is at least medium risk; recognizable unlicensed characters or copied artwork can be high or extreme.
